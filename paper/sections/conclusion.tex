\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This work defines a reproducible score-level workflow for neural-symbolic post-tonal composition assistance. A legal procedural corpus, condition-preserving Transformer training, explicit pitch-class-set and serial analysis, grammar-constrained candidate generation, MusicXML export, and per-fragment reports are integrated in one local pipeline.

The corrected protocol is designed to distinguish sequence modeling, inference-time reranking, condition ablation, held-out rhythmic evaluation, and notation adherence. Its scientific claims will remain limited to automatic controllability on the synthetic testbed until the full v3 experiment completes. Human evaluation and independent legal MusicXML validation are required before drawing conclusions about artistic usefulness.
