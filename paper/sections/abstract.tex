\begin{abstract}
Explicit control of post-tonal material remains underrepresented in symbolic music generation, while evaluation on copyrighted contemporary repertoire is difficult to release and reproduce. We present a score-level neural-symbolic composition assistant conditioned on pitch-class sets and interval vectors, optional twelve-tone rows and serial transformations, rhythmic profiles, gestures, instrumentation, voice count, and requested score span. A legal procedural generator supplies an explicit corpus split of 20,000 training, 2,000 validation, and 2,000 test fragments. A causal Transformer learns condition-preserving event windows, and inference-time symbolic analysis reranks sampled candidates before MusicXML and a JSON report are exported. The corrected evaluation separates serial row targets from non-serial pitch-class-set targets, hides stochastic density targets from decoding, evaluates condition ablations against the original held-out targets, and checks requested measure and voice counts in exported scores. Corrected full neural metrics are \texttt{PENDING\_REAL\_EXPERIMENT} until every configured checkpoint and evaluation gate completes. The present manuscript therefore specifies the auditable method and claim boundaries without substituting smoke or legacy results for the formal experiment.
\end{abstract}
