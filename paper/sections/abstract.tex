\begin{abstract}
We present a runnable research codebase for AI-assisted contemporary art music composition at the symbolic score level. The system combines legal synthetic post-tonal corpus generation, pitch-class set and interval-vector analysis, twelve-tone row transformations, rhythmic-profile conditioning, gesture labels, MusicXML export, and a lightweight Transformer trained on event tokens. The method is framed as neural-symbolic constraint-guided decoding rather than audio synthesis. Quantitative tables are populated only by completed local runs; otherwise values remain marked as PENDING\_REAL\_EXPERIMENT.
\end{abstract}
