\section{Related Work}

Computer-assisted composition environments such as OpenMusic provide symbolic workflows for compositional reasoning. Pitch-class set theory and serial procedures provide analytical vocabulary for post-tonal materials. Symbolic music generation with Transformer models demonstrates sequence modeling capacity, while neural-symbolic approaches motivate explicit constraint checks and guided decoding.

This work differs from audio generation and popular-song accompaniment models by focusing on contemporary score fragments, MusicXML output, and explainable constraint metrics.
