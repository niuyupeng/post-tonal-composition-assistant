\section{Limitations}

The corpus is synthetic by design. This improves legality and reproducibility but limits claims about historical style, idiomatic notation, and composer-specific practice. The current score representation is intentionally compact and does not yet model all contemporary notation, extended techniques, articulation detail, beaming, tuplets, or page layout.

Transformer-generated examples should be interpreted as symbolic sketches. The project does not generate audio and does not claim perceptual realism. Full experimental claims require completed local runs and, ideally, blind expert ratings.
