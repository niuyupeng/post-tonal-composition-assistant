## Data availability

Training, validation, and test material is generated procedurally from fixed
seeds; no copyrighted post-1945 score is required. The completed formal split
contains 20,000 training, 2,000 validation, and 2,000 test fragments with
`smoke=false`. Canonical aggregate metrics, constraint metrics, per-example
records, LaTeX tables, and twenty proposed-model MusicXML examples are present
in the repository. Large processed tensors and checkpoints are reproducible
from saved configurations, hashes, and seeds. The archival URL and DOI are
PENDING_RELEASE.

## Ethics and consent

The reported automatic experiments use only procedurally generated symbolic
data and involve no human participants or personal data. Blank blind-rating
forms are included, but no ratings were collected or analyzed. Any later expert
study must follow the institutional determination and consent process
appropriate to its protocol.
