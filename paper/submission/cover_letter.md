Dear Editors of the *Journal of New Music Research*,

Please consider our manuscript, “Neural-Symbolic Post-Tonal Composition Assistance with Pitch-Class Set, Serial, and Rhythmic-Profile Constraints,” for publication in the *Journal of New Music Research*.

The manuscript reports a score-level system for post-tonal composition assistance. A causal Transformer receives pitch-class-set, serial, rhythmic-profile, gesture, instrumentation, voice-count, and span conditions. At inference, explicit symbolic diagnostics rerank sampled candidates before the system exports MusicXML and a per-score analysis report. The study uses a reproducible rule-generated corpus rather than copyrighted post-1945 scores.

The primary experiment is a controlled same-checkpoint comparison on aligned test requests, followed by the same paired protocol on three independently trained checkpoints. It evaluates whether finite-candidate reranking changes the diagnostics it was designed to optimize and reports both recurring favorable changes and recurring constraint conflicts. The manuscript does not claim stylistic imitation, artistic superiority, or composer preference. We believe this bounded combination of computer-assisted composition, post-tonal analysis, symbolic generation, and inspectable score output fits the journal's scientific and technological music-research remit.

The code, configurations, corpus generator, evaluation outputs, and MusicXML examples are maintained in a reproducible repository. An anonymized review snapshot or archival release will be supplied according to the journal's current policy.
