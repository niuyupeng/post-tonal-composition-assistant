Dear Editors of the *Journal of New Music Research*,

Please consider our manuscript, "Neural-Symbolic Post-Tonal Composition
Assistant with Pitch-Class Set, Serial, and Rhythmic-Profile Constraints," for
publication in the *Journal of New Music Research*.

The manuscript presents a score-level system for post-tonal composition
assistance. A causal Transformer is conditioned on pitch-class sets, serial
forms, rhythmic profiles, gestures, instrumentation, voice count, and score
span. Sampled scores are assessed by explicit symbolic diagnostics before
MusicXML and per-score analysis reports are exported. The study uses a
reproducible rule-generated corpus rather than copyrighted post-1945 scores.

The formal experiment uses a 20,000/2,000/2,000 split and evaluates thirteen
configurations on 2,000 test conditions each. With one shared checkpoint,
increasing the candidate budget from one to four and applying symbolic scoring
raised pc-set coverage from 0.9963 to 0.9999 and cyclic row-order accuracy from
0.1581 to 0.2411, while reducing interval-vector distance from 0.2390 to
0.0045. Matching condition-removal experiments degraded the corresponding
automatic endpoints. All 260 attempted MusicXML exports passed structural,
measure-count, and part-count checks. The manuscript also reports the principal
negative results: exact serial-form accuracy remained zero for all neural
configurations, and generated content occupied about half of the requested span
on average.

The manuscript does not claim stylistic imitation, artistic superiority, or
composer preference. Its intended contribution is a reproducible and
inspectable workflow connecting computer-assisted composition, post-tonal
analysis, symbolic generation, and editable score output.
